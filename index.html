Hermes

Hermes is a personal AI assistant used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

It accesses the owner's own Google account (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Sheets, Docs, Contacts) to help organize files, schedules and email.

Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Hermes accesses Google user data only for the owner's personal use. Data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Access tokens are stored privately and can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Hermes's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
